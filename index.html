MailClient

A personal Gmail app for macOS.

MailClient is a small native Mac app that reads and sends Gmail through the Gmail API. One person wrote it for their own use. It is not sold, offered or supported for anyone else.

What it does

  • Shows the owner's Gmail inboxes, threads and labels.
  • Archives, deletes, labels and stars mail.
  • Writes and sends mail as Gmail drafts.
  • Gets new-mail updates through Google Cloud Pub/Sub.

Where your data goes

Mail stays in Gmail and on the owner's Mac. The app talks only to Google. It has no server of its own and sends no data anywhere else.
