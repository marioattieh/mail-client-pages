Mario Mail MCP

Personal Gmail tools for one person.

Mario Mail MCP is the Google sign-in for two small tools. One person wrote them for their own Google accounts. They are not sold, offered or supported for anyone else.

The tools

  • A Mac mail app. It shows the owner's Gmail, archives, deletes, labels and stars mail, and sends mail as Gmail drafts. It gets new-mail updates through Google Cloud Pub/Sub.
  • A Gmail tool for Claude. It runs on the owner's Mac. When the owner asks Claude, Anthropic's AI assistant, about their mail, it reads that mail from Gmail and writes drafts.

Where your data goes

Mail stays in Gmail and on the owner's Mac. The mail app talks only to Google. The Claude tool also gives Claude the mail the owner asks about. Neither tool has a server of its own.
