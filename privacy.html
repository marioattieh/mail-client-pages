Privacy policy

Mario Mail MCP · Last updated 26 November 2026

Mario Mail MCP is the Google sign-in for two personal tools: a Mac mail app and a Gmail tool for Claude. Only their owner uses them, with the owner's own Google accounts.

What the tools get from Google

  • Your email address, to know which account is signed in.
  • Your Gmail messages, labels, drafts and signatures.
  • New-mail notices, through Google Cloud Pub/Sub (mail app only).

Where it is kept

  • The mail app keeps a copy of recent mail in a database on the owner's Mac, inside the app's sandbox.
  • Sign-in tokens are kept on the owner's Mac.
  • Neither tool keeps your data on a server of its own.

Who gets it

  • The mail app talks only to Google's servers.
  • The Claude tool gives Claude, Anthropic's AI assistant, only the mail the owner asks about. Anthropic handles it under the owner's own Anthropic account.
  • Neither tool sells or shares your data with anyone else. They have no ads, analytics or tracking.

Remote images in a message stay blocked in the mail app until you choose to load them. If you do, they come from the sender's servers, as in any mail app.

How to remove access

Remove the account in the mail app's Settings, or remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the mail app's sandbox folder deletes its local copy of your mail.
