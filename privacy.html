Privacy policy

MailClient · Last updated 26 November 2026

MailClient is a personal macOS app. Only its owner uses it, with the owner's own Google accounts.

What the app gets from Google

  • Your email address, to know which account is signed in.
  • Your Gmail messages, labels, drafts and signatures, through the gmail.modify scope.
  • New-mail notices, through Google Cloud Pub/Sub.

Where it is kept

  • A copy of recent mail is kept in a database on the owner's Mac, inside the app's sandbox.
  • Sign-in tokens are kept in the macOS Keychain.
  • Nothing is kept on any other computer or server.

Who gets it

No one. The app talks only to Google's servers. It has no server of its own. It does not sell, share or send your data to anyone. It has no ads, analytics or tracking.

Remote images in a message stay blocked until you choose to load them. If you do, they come from the sender's servers, as in any mail app.

Google API Services

MailClient's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How to remove access

Remove the account in the app's Settings, or remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's sandbox folder deletes the local copy of your mail.
